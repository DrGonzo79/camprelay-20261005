## Useful summarized details

The source frames camp enrollment as a high-stress timed-opening problem. Its example brief asks for soccer in July under $400 with aftercare and science as backup. The proposed flow is parent approval of camps, budget, and payment followed by monitoring and submission when enrollment opens. It suggests starting with one connector, then expanding to parent-focused connectors, media, and community.

The email graphic attributes two figures to 2022 American Camp Association enrollment research: 40% of surveyed parents ranked signup ease among their top three considerations, and 84% wanted waitlist alerts. It also points to an advertised Texas concierge service around $500 per child. These are source-provided research leads, not independently reproduced evidence. The prototype therefore labels all programs fictional and avoids market-size or revenue claims.

## Product interpretation

CampRelay narrows the concept to shortlist ranking, readiness, monitoring, and official-form handoff. This makes the core job testable while avoiding unapproved payment APIs, credential handling, bot evasion, and claims of autonomous registration.
