# CEO review

## Challenge

The original premise jumps from painful registration to autonomous form submission and then to a broad parent media/community business. That hides three businesses: local data operations, browser automation, and media. The user pain is credible, but the frequency is seasonal; a generic connector risks high breakage and low recurring revenue. The source’s research and advertised competitor price improve the case, yet neither proves payment for this exact wedge.

## Decisions after review

1. **Wedge narrowed:** one-city Registration Relay, not universal auto-registration.
2. **Buyer sharpened:** the parent accountable for childcare coverage, not “parents” broadly.
3. **CTA priced:** test a refundable $49 seasonal deposit against explicit success/kill thresholds.
4. **Automation constrained:** monitor and prepare; always hand off to the official form. No credentials or payment in the prototype.
5. **GTM localized:** opening calendar and preflight distributed through parent groups/PTOs before a newsletter empire.
6. **Economics instrumented:** support minutes and data freshness are gating metrics because local operations can erase margin.
7. **Moat clarified:** normalized facts, change history, source reliability, and outcomes—not an AI wrapper.
8. **Architecture corrected:** deterministic ranking, adapter isolation, auditability, idempotent alerts, and explicit degraded states.

## Failure modes to watch

Parents may accept reminders but reject payment; source details may change too frequently; high-demand camps may prohibit automation; the alert can shift rather than remove the final scramble; aftercare and eligibility rules can be misread; acquisition may depend on moderators or local partnerships. Any claim of a successful signup must come from parent confirmation, never from an alert being sent.

## Verdict

**Build as a demand and operational-learning prototype.** Do not invest in autonomous purchasing or multi-city coverage until five of ten qualified parents pay and first-city freshness/support thresholds pass.
