# Office Hours diagnostic

## Specific customer

Dual-income parents of children ages 5–12 in one metro area who coordinate multiple summer programs, need aftercare, and face timed registration openings. The economic buyer is the parent managing the family calendar—not camps, schools, or a generic “busy family.”

## Painful status quo

The parent keeps tabs, calendars, passwords, child details, payment methods, and backup programs across incompatible camp sites. When registration opens, missing information or a sold-out primary choice forces rushed tradeoffs. The source email compares the scramble to high-demand ticket sales and cites 2022 research saying signup ease and waitlist alerts matter; those claims have not been independently validated here.

## Desperate use case

“Find a July soccer camp under $400 with aftercare, hold science as the backup, and make sure I am ready when enrollment opens.” The acute moment is 48 hours before a known opening, when a parent will pay to avoid losing both the preferred camp and childcare coverage.

## Narrowest paid wedge

One-city, one-season **Registration Relay**: a human-reviewed shortlist, opening-time monitor, preflight checklist, and parent-approved handoff to the official form. Test **$49 per child/season**. Do not begin with universal browser automation, credential storage, or autonomous purchasing; those multiply liability and breakage before demand is proven.

## Observation and validation

1. Observe 10 parents planning the next summer season; count tabs, missing fields, late discoveries, and backup changes.
2. Concierge 10 registration relays using public program details and manual reminders.
3. Ask for a refundable $49 deposit only after showing the exact shortlist and monitoring plan.
4. Success: 5/10 parents pay and at least 4 use an alert or readiness packet at opening time.
5. Kill/reshape: fewer than 2 deposits, or parents consistently prefer a calendar template over monitoring.

## Future-fit

If the wedge earns trust, expand to classes, childcare waitlists, school applications, and family-admin connectors. The durable asset would be normalized registration rules and observed opening reliability—not a generic AI chat layer.
